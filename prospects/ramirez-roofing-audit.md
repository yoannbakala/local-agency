## Website Assessment
**No website exists.** This is the #1 gap.

A homeowner searching "roofing contractor Harvey IL" will find Ramirez on Google Maps — but if they want to learn more before calling, there's nowhere to go. They'll click through to a competitor who *does* have a site.

## Strengths
1. **Exceptional reputation** — 4.9 stars across 48 Google reviews is elite for a local roofer
2. **BBB A+ with zero complaints** — strong trust signal once surfaced properly
3. **Licensed, bonded, insured** — credentials in place, just not visible on a website
4. **Review quotes are gold** — "great service, workmanship and communication," "very thorough estimate and reasonable prices," "customer service is second to none" — these sell themselves

## Problems Found
1. **No website** — losing every prospect who Google-searches then wants to research before calling
2. **Facebook barely active** — 160 followers, only 3 reviews, unclear posting cadence
3. **Yelp underutilized** — only 4 reviews vs 48 on Google; photos exist but no owner responses visible
4. **No way to request estimates online** — phone-only means lost leads after hours and on Sundays
5. **Reviews not leveraged** — 48 five-star reviews sitting on Google with no website to showcase them
6. **No service area pages** — missing SEO for "roofer in Dolton," "roof repair South Holland," etc.

## Opportunities
1. **Website = immediate credibility lift.** A 5-page site with their real reviews, services, and a contact form would convert browsers into callers
2. **Local SEO landgrab** — competitors have sites targeting Harvey/Dolton/South Holland/Calumet City keywords; Ramirez has zero organic search presence beyond Maps
3. **After-hours lead capture** — a contact form catches the 6PM homeowner who just noticed a leak
4. **Review funnel** — add a "Leave a Review" link to turn happy customers into Google reviews faster (48 is good; 100+ dominates)
